# A five-minute FINN walkthrough

## 0:00 — Start with attention

Open Briefing on a phone. Explain the visible dates: market prices are real saved EOD records; filing cards are a historical evidence library. Show the category filter, recent-window filter and collapsed routine item. An empty recent window is an honest result, not a failed demo.

Open the first Infosys insight. Review Revenue, EBIT, PBT and PAT, the QoQ/YoY calculations, currency and reporting basis. Open the original fact sheet. Save the item and return to Saved.

## 1:00 — Follow a movement

Open Portfolio. Select a holding and switch chart ranges. Inspect a date with the slider. Toggle volume and filing markers. Read the calculated price return and volume ratio; do not call this an explanation of the price movement. Refresh the provider data; if it fails, the dated snapshot remains visible.

## 2:00 — Test a management promise

Open Research → Infosys → Promises vs. results. Explain that the original FY2026 guidance and the FY2026 outcome use the same full-year period. Inspect both primary sources. Point out that intervening guidance revisions are not covered in this first version.

Ask: “Compare the original FY2026 revenue-growth guidance with delivery. What information is missing to judge later revisions?”

The answer should cite the management transcript and results source, distinguish original from revised guidance, and expose missing information.

## 3:00 — Try a company outside the demo set

Enter WIPRO or another valid NSE ticker. Discover primary reports. If the publisher is unavailable, paste a short extract from a company annual report or earnings transcript, including the period and page information. Ask a question about only those facts. FINN must not produce an unrelated prewritten dossier.

An extraction demonstration can use this explicitly fictional test input, labelled as a test rather than real company data:

> Test document for pipeline validation, not a real issuer disclosure. For the year ended 31 March 2025, revenue was ₹100 crore and profit after tax was ₹12 crore. Prior-year revenue was ₹80 crore. The document contains no debt figure.

Ask: “What was revenue growth, and what can you establish about debt?”

Expected: 25% growth; debt cannot be established. The extract is user-provided and must be labelled accordingly.

## 4:00 — Make the workflow personal

Add a thesis-watch reason with company and keywords. Inspect matched disclosures. Explain that matching indicates relevance, not an automated investment verdict.

Open Alerts, lower the price threshold and watch the matching signal count update. Reload to confirm preferences persist. Alerts run in-app on the latest available EOD records; there are no background notifications yet.

### Daily review and follow-ups

On Briefing, click **Refresh watchlist** and check the price-session dates and refresh result. Click **Mark reviewed** to store the baseline in this browser. On a later trading session, refresh again: newer sessions are compared against that baseline, with the six largest absolute changes linked to research. Mark reviewed again when finished. A refresh may return cached data or a dated fallback; it does not guarantee a newer market session.

In Notebook, add a follow-up date to any note. Use Due/Open/Resolved to triage research, and Mark resolved or Reopen question to update its status. Exports preserve dates, status, sources and text. No email or background reminders are sent.

### Company-name search and the market desk

Search **Wipro** and choose the NSE listing. Check the currency, price range controls, automatically calculated movement and volume observations, dated exchange disclosures, and original annual-report/transcript links. Try **Toyota** to see multiple exchanges, and **Apple** to see USD pricing. Source providers can refuse access; the interface shows that gap rather than inventing financial analysis. The research button uses Google-authenticated, source-grounded AI.

On Briefing, switch the featured company chart, open research or compare from the market stage, and pause/resume the dated market ribbon. With the operating system’s reduced-motion preference enabled, the ribbon and ornamental transitions are static. On small screens the chart coordinate width adapts to keep labels legible.
